# Brian Phase 3.8 — Global Sensor Mesh & Opportunity Tournament

## Scope

Phase 3.8 turns Brian from a small fixed trade universe into a central research brain surrounded by scalable logical specialist eyes. It is **PROSPECTIVE_DEVELOPMENT_SHADOW** only.

This phase does **not** change or contaminate the running Phase 3.7 frozen 7-day live-shadow control experiment. Phase 3.7 remains unchanged and learning-disabled.

## Logical eyes, not thousands of processes

A `SensorTemplate` is expanded over assets to create deterministic `LogicalEye` identities. Ten crypto templates over 70 assets create 700 logical eyes without starting 700 operating-system processes. The same mechanism can later expand across FX, commodities, equities, ETFs, rates and macro series.

Initial logical specialist families:

- price / chart structure
- relative momentum
- mean reversion / exhaustion
- order-book liquidity
- derivatives / funding / open interest / liquidation
- on-chain economic whale flow
- verified news events
- social psychology / panic / greed propagation
- cross-asset confirmation
- macro / rates / FX liquidity context

A registered template is not proof that its data source is live. If evidence is unavailable, the eye must emit `available=false`, `direction=0`. Missing evidence is never fabricated.

## Virtual specialist micro-books

Each eye may be evaluated with a preregistered virtual micro-book ticket of exactly:

- $2
- $3
- $5
- $10
- $20

These are statistical research accounts, not exchange balances. Every receipt records net PnL, trading costs, turnover, active decisions, wins/losses and maximum drawdown. No live order method or exchange credential is introduced.

This allows Brian to learn which specialist families repeatedly create cost-adjusted value and which merely produce noise.

## Opportunity Tournament

Sensor observations are grouped by asset, horizon and PIT decision time. One evidence family cannot manufacture consensus by emitting many duplicate agents: only the strongest observation from each `independent_group` contributes to the tournament candidate.

The tournament preserves conflicting evidence and requires multiple independent evidence groups before a candidate is eligible by default. Eligible candidates receive only virtual tickets. Total virtual allocation is capped at $500 and remains unlevered.

The tournament is a research ranking layer. It cannot place a live order or promote a model.

## Missed Opportunity Ledger

Phase 3.8 reserves an append-only ledger for prospective missed-opportunity diagnostics. After a horizon resolves, it can record whether Brian was WAIT / BUY / SELL / OUT_OF_UNIVERSE and what the subsequent path actually did, including cost-adjusted return, MFE and MAE when causally available.

This is hindsight diagnostics, not a trading signal. It exists to answer:

- Did Brian correctly avoid noise?
- Did Brian miss a repeatable opportunity?
- Was the asset outside its current execution universe?
- Which sensor family saw the move early?
- Which missing sensor would have been needed?

## Market breadth roadmap

The mesh is provider-neutral. Planned source domains are:

- Crypto spot universe and chart structure
- Crypto derivatives
- DEX/on-chain and whale entity flows
- News/event truth and source reputation
- X/Reddit/social propagation and manipulation detection
- FX
- Gold, silver, oil, copper and other commodities
- Equities and ETFs through an appropriate licensed data provider
- Rates and macro context through official/revision-aware sources where possible

Different markets keep their native clocks and trading calendars. Daily/monthly macro or commodity observations are joined causally as-of; they are never fabricated into fake 5-minute bars.

## Capital rule

The user's $500 benchmark remains a virtual research account. Hundreds or thousands of eyes do not mean hundreds or thousands of simultaneous funded positions. Evidence is filtered, deduplicated and ranked before the central allocator considers it.

Real capital, authenticated exchange execution, leverage, auto-promotion and self-modifying live code remain out of scope.
